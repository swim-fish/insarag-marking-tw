    <h1 class="entry-title">INSARAG GUIDELINES</h1>  </div><!-- .entry-header-inner -->
</header><!-- .entry-header -->

          <div class="post-inner thin ">
            <div class="entry-content">
              		<div data-elementor-type="wp-post" data-elementor-id="13" class="elementor elementor-13" data-elementor-post-type="post">
				<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-88e2f5d e-flex e-con-boxed e-con e-parent" data-id="88e2f5d" data-element_type="container" data-e-type="container">
					<div class="e-con-inner">
				<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-084e356 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="084e356" data-element_type="widget" data-e-type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
				<div class="elementor-widget-container">
									<p>We are pleased to announce that the new INSARAG Guidelines were officially launched during the INSARAG Global Meeting in Foz do Iguaçu on 9<sup>th</sup> July and are now available online.</p><p>Access the guidelines:</p><ul><li>INSARAG website</li><li>Dedicated guidelines platform: <a href="https://insarag-guidelines.netlify.app" target="_blank" rel="noopener">https://insarag-guidelines.netlify.app</a></li></ul><div><b> </b></div><div><b>The guidelines will be effective as of 1st January 2027. (For reference the current  version &#8211; Guidelines 2020 &#8211; is available below).</b></div><div><b> </b></div><p>This milestone would not have been possible without the dedication, expertise, and collaboration of the many individuals who contributed to the review and development process. We extend our sincere thanks to everyone involved, and especially to the members of the <strong>Guidelines Review Group</strong>, whose hard work and commitment were instrumental in delivering this new version.</p><p>We encourage all INSARAG stakeholders and partners to explore the updated guidelines and support their implementation across the global USAR community.</p>								</div>
				</div>
					</div>
				</div>
		<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-8b4ef8c e-flex e-con-boxed e-con e-parent" data-id="8b4ef8c" data-element_type="container" data-e-type="container">
					<div class="e-con-inner">
				<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-9e8a666 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="9e8a666" data-element_type="widget" data-e-type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
				<div class="elementor-widget-container">
									<h3><a href="https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1c3Sp5UZ_lxg5vWVe3oXRpNsPntvUll0b?usp=drive_link" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Volume 1 &#8211; Policy</a></h3><h3><a href="https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1jP_jDRcehDrI8AQKEonx5M8z7Zron4mA?usp=drive_link" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Volume 2 &#8211; National Capacity Building</a></h3><h3><a href="https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1DDQQa7h-wwr82dW9CFqRszN7W3Rk8W8X?usp=drive_link" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Volume 3 &#8211; Operations</a></h3><h3><a href="https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1TKq41XCr4C9QFavBOT9KxWAFJF4sP9_W?usp=drive_link" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Volume 4 &#8211; IEC-R</a></h3><h3><a href="https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1SiZmN9US2vL323wZ18Xmkw3nY-TBf56A?usp=drive_link" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Volume 5 &#8211; Operational Field Guide</a></h3><p> </p>								</div>
				</div>
					</div>
				</div>
		<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-6d14780e e-flex e-con-boxed e-con e-parent" data-id="6d14780e" data-element_type="container" data-e-type="container">
					<div class="e-con-inner">
				<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-104a45fd elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="104a45fd" data-element_type="widget" data-e-type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
				<div class="elementor-widget-container">
									<p><b>For your reference &#8211;Guidelines 2020</b></p><p>The INSARAG Guidelines provides a methodology to guide countries affected by a sudden-onset disaster causing large-scale structural collapse, as well as international USAR Teams responding in the affected country. The Guidelines also outlines the role of the UN in assisting affected countries in on-site coordination.</p><p>Reviewed and updated between 2018 to 2020, the INSARAG Guidelines 2020 incorporates six new areas from the 2015 version that were endorsed by the ISG; (i) IER Pre-Greening Process, (ii) INSARAG Recognised National Accreditation Process (IRNAP), (iii) USAR Coordination Cell (UCC), (iv) Information Management, (v) Classified Light Teams and (vi) Beyond the Rubble.</p><p><b>2020 &#8211; Volume I –</b><b> Policy </b><a href="https://insarag.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/INSARAG20Guidelines20Vol20I.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><b>Link</b></a></p><p><b>2020 &#8211; Volume</b><b> II, Manual A – Capacity </b><b>Building</b><a href="https://insarag.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/INSARAG20Guidelines20Vol20II2C20Man20A.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><b>Link</b></a></p><p><b>2020 &#8211; Volume</b><b> II, Manual B – Operations </b><a href="https://insarag.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/INSARAG20Guidelines20Vol20II2C20Man20B.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><b>Link</b></a></p><p><b>2020 &#8211; Volume</b><b> II, Manual C – INSARAG External Classification &amp; Reclassification </b><a href="https://insarag.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/INSARAG20Guidelines20Vol20II2C20Man20C.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><b>Link</b></a></p><p><b>2020 &#8211; Volume</b><b> III – Operational Field Guide <a href="https://insarag.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/INSARAG20Guidelines20Vol20III.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Link</a></b></p><p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Note</span>: INSARAG endorsed manuals, selected annexes and checklists have been reorganized and can now be found in the <a href="https://www.insarag.org/guidance-notes/guidance-notes/">Guidance Notes section</a>. <b></b></p><p>Other reference notes can be found in the <a href="https://www.insarag.org/technical-reference-library/index/">Technical Reference Library</a>.</p><p>You can find the current Guidelines 2020 with their annexes at the following link: <a href="https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1Wp38Kfhy7pG8mBYNYxlOWLgl2tnw1zRM?usp=sharing">Link</a></p><p><img fetchpriority="high" decoding="async" class="wp-image-1047 alignnone" src="https://insarag.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/Diagram-300x138.jpg" alt="Diagram describing the Guidelines" width="580" height="267" srcset="https://insarag.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/Diagram-300x138.jpg 300w, https://insarag.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/Diagram-768x353.jpg 768w, https://insarag.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/Diagram.JPG 783w" sizes="(max-width: 580px) 100vw, 580px" /></p><p>The INSARAG Secretariat welcomes the Members States and donors to translate the Guidelines into other languages for greater outreach.</p><p>Please find below the Guidelines in other languages:</p><p><a
