type="button" class="clean-btn tocCollapsibleButton_TO0P">On this page</button></div><div class="theme-doc-markdown markdown"><header><h1>Assessment, Search and Rescue (ASR) Levels</h1></header><p>A key element of the <a class="" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/coordination-elements"><ins>INSARAG coordination</ins></a> are the definitions of key types of work
typically needed during a major <span class="term" data-tooltip="Urban Search and Rescue">USAR</span> incident. This can range from initial assessment of
the affected area to deconstructing a building to recover the last deceased victim.</p>
<p>Having a clear definition of all the possible operational levels allows the coordination actors
to be specific about the planning, tasking, specific <span class="term" data-tooltip="Urban Search and Rescue">USAR</span> operations needed and the progress made.
Information management tools (templates, forms, reports, marking system, <span class="term" data-tooltip="Virtual On-Site Operations Coordination Centre">VOSOCC</span> etc.) used to
facilitate coordination also relate to the level of USAR work being carried out. <a class="term" data-tooltip="INSARAG Coordination and Management System" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/icms">ICMS</a> is used to facilitate coordination, it relates to the level of
USAR work needed to be carried out.</p>
<p>USAR operations are divided into the five <span class="term" data-tooltip="Assessment, Search and Rescue">ASR</span> levels. These levels are defined as sequential
activities, but, in reality, teams may receive assignments in any order. Especially in large
scale operations where <a class="term" data-tooltip="Local Emergency Management Authority" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/coordination-elements#local-emergency-management-authority-lema">LEMA</a> identifies worksites before teams arrive. Or when new areas are
being opened throughout the response. Hence different levels of work are being carried
out in different areas of the incident at the same moment.</p>
<p>The five <span class="term" data-tooltip="Assessment, Search and Rescue">ASR</span> levels are identified as:</p>
<ul>
<li class=""><strong>ASR Level 1:</strong> Wide Area Assessment</li>
<li class=""><strong>ASR Level 2:</strong> Worksite Triage Assessment</li>
<li class=""><strong>ASR Level 3:</strong> Rapid Search and Rescue</li>
<li class=""><strong>ASR Level 4:</strong> Full Search and Rescue</li>
<li class=""><strong>ASR Level 5:</strong> Total Coverage Search and Recovery</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="anchor anchorTargetStickyNavbar_Vzrq" id="asr-level-1-wide-area-assessment">ASR Level 1: Wide Area Assessment<a href="#asr-level-1-wide-area-assessment" class="hash-link" aria-label="Direct link to ASR Level 1: Wide Area Assessment" title="Direct link to ASR Level 1: Wide Area Assessment" translate="no">​</a></h2>
<p><strong>Definitions and purpose</strong></p>
<ul>
<li class="">The preliminary survey of the affected or assigned area</li>
<li class="">For the purpose of:<!-- -->
<ul>
<li class="">Determining the scope and magnitude of the incident</li>
<li class="">Identifying scope, location and types of damage</li>
<li class="">Estimating the urgent resource needs</li>
<li class="">Developing a <a class="" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/sectorisation-and-zones"><ins>Sectorisation</ins></a> plan</li>
<li class="">Establishing priorities</li>
<li class="">Identifying general hazards</li>
<li class="">Identifying infrastructure issues</li>
<li class="">Identifying potential <span class="term" data-tooltip="Base of Operations">BoO</span> locations</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li class="">Usually accomplished by; vehicle, helicopter, waterborne craft, on foot or from reports from
others e.g. the <a class="term" data-tooltip="Local Emergency Management Authority" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/coordination-elements#local-emergency-management-authority-lema">LEMA</a></li>
<li class="">Initial, fast visual check of the damaged or assigned area</li>
</ul>
<p>Teams carrying out this level of assessment must remain mobile, not engage in rescue operations
and report the results as quickly as possible.</p>
<p><strong>Carried out when and by who</strong></p>
<ol>
<li class="">The <a class="term" data-tooltip="Local Emergency Management Authority" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/coordination-elements#local-emergency-management-authority-lema">LEMA</a> often do this prior to the arrival of teams and provide all or some of this information<br>
<!-- -->(If it is not complete it may be beneficial to redo this)</li>
<li class="">Can be done by members of the <span class="term" data-tooltip="On-Site Operations Coordination Centre">OSOCC</span>/<span class="term" data-tooltip="UN Disaster Assessment and Coordination">UNDAC</span> team on their arrival</li>
<li class=""><span class="term" data-tooltip="Urban Search and Rescue">USAR</span> teams</li>
</ol>
<p><strong><span class="term" data-tooltip="International Search and Rescue Advisory Group">INSARAG</span> Tools</strong></p>
<ol>
<li class="">Information on the <span class="term" data-tooltip="Virtual On-Site Operations Coordination Centre">VOSOCC</span></li>
<li class=""><a class="term" data-tooltip="Reception/Departure Centre" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/rdc">RDC</a>/OSOCC briefing</li>
<li class="">These could be supported by information such as: LEMA briefings, maps, GPS coordinates,
photographs, and video</li>
<li class="">
<a class="term" data-tooltip="INSARAG Coordination and Management System" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/icms">ICMS</a>
</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Outputs</strong></p>
<ol>
<li class=""><span class="term" data-tooltip="On-Site Operations Coordination Centre">OSOCC</span>/<a class="term" data-tooltip="Reception/Departure Centre" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/rdc">RDC</a>/<a class="term" data-tooltip="USAR Coordination Cell" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/ucc">UCC</a> briefing</li>
<li class=""><a class="" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/sectorisation-and-zones"><ins>Sectorisation</ins></a> plan</li>
<li class=""><span class="term" data-tooltip="Base of Operations">BoO</span> location(s)</li>
<li class="">Initial priorities and plan</li>
<li class="">Resource requests e.g. more teams</li>
<li class="">Posts on the <span class="term" data-tooltip="Virtual On-Site Operations Coordination Centre">VOSOCC</span></li>
<li class="">Updates on the ICMS dashboard</li>
</ol>
<h2 class="anchor anchorTargetStickyNavbar_Vzrq" id="asr-level-2-worksite-triage-assessment">ASR Level 2: Worksite Triage Assessment<a href="#asr-level-2-worksite-triage-assessment" class="hash-link" aria-label="Direct link to ASR Level 2: Worksite Triage Assessment" title="Direct link to ASR Level 2: Worksite Triage Assessment" translate="no">​</a></h2>
<p><strong>Definitions and purpose</strong></p>
<ul>
<li class="">The main purpose is to identify specific and viable live rescue sites within the allocated
sector to allow assignment prioritisation and make a plan of action</li>
<li class="">It needs to be a fast paced but methodical assessment</li>
<li class="">The aim is to assess the whole sector in a timely manner</li>
<li class="">The <a class="" href="/operations/operational-procedures/worksite-triage#worksite-triage-form"><ins>Worksite triage form</ins></a> must be used to gather the essential information at this stage</li>
<li class="">Information from the local population and local responders is often valuable and should be
sought during the assessment</li>
<li class="">Rescues are not usually performed during this level unless an unexpected opportunity arises</li>
<li class="">If live victims are found the decision on whether the assessment team stays to start the rescue
or carries on the assessment will be dependent on the situation and the brief the team received,
some options are:<!-- -->
<ul>
<li class="">Create a worksite form</li>
<li class="">Additional resources are called in to carry out the rescue via the <a class="term" data-tooltip="USAR Coordination Cell" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/ucc">UCC</a>/SCC. The Assessment
team stays but must ensure the sector assessment continues simultaneously, keeping <a class="term" data-tooltip="USAR Coordination Cell" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/ucc">UCC</a>/SCC
advised of progress/delays</li>
<li class="">Assessment is completed by others as soon as possible, request through UCC/SCC</li>
<li class="">Adopt a strategy to send a combined team able to do both ASR Level 2 sector Assessment and ASR
Level 3 Rapid Search and Rescue (split squad methodology)</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li class="">An ASR Level 2 Assessment can be repeated later if it is thought necessary, e.g. a nighttime
assessment or an assessment with additional dogs, which may produce different results</li>
<li class="">In this phase it may be decided to switch to rapid response if the possible number of worksites
strongly exceeds the available operational capacity (in the sector)</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Carried out when and by who</strong></p>
<ol>
<li class="">It is preferably done closely behind the Level 1 Wide Area Assessment and as soon as possible
after sectors have been established</li>
<li class="">The <a class="term" data-tooltip="Local Emergency Management Authority" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/coordination-elements#local-emergency-management-authority-lema">LEMA</a> may have sectorised and started this process prior to assistance arriving. If this is
not complete it may be beneficial for a <span class="term" data-tooltip="Urban Search and Rescue">USAR</span> team to redo this</li>
<li class="">If the <a class="term" data-tooltip="Local Emergency Management Authority" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/coordination-elements#local-emergency-management-authority-lema">LEMA</a> has not done this, then it should be the first action of the initial <span class="term" data-tooltip="Urban Search and Rescue">USAR</span>
team(s) in a sector</li>
<li class="">USAR teams</li>
<li class="">Use of dogs or technical search equipment is optional and will depend on the situation. Using these
will improve the detailed results but will slow down the process; a balance is needed</li>
</ol>
<p><strong><span class="term" data-tooltip="International Search and Rescue Advisory Group">INSARAG</span> Tools</strong></p>
<ol>
<li class=""><a class="" href="/operations/operational-procedures/worksite-triage#worksite-triage-form"><ins>Worksite triage form</ins></a></li>
<li class="">Briefing from <a class="term" data-tooltip="USAR Coordination Cell" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/ucc">UCC</a></li>
<li class="">A map of the sector area being assessed is highly recommended and should be used to clarify the
areas assessed and cleared
(There could also be information such as: LEMA briefings, information from local teams, GPS
coordinates, photographs etc.)</li>
<li class="">
<a class="term" data-tooltip="INSARAG Coordination and Management System" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/icms">ICMS</a>
</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Outputs</strong></p>
<ol>
<li class="">Completed <a class="" href="/operations/operational-procedures/worksite-triage#worksite-triage-form"><ins>Worksite triage form</ins></a> identifying viable worksites.</li>
<li class="">A completed <a class="" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/sectorisation-and-zones#worksite-identification"><ins>worksite ID</ins></a>.</li>
<li class="">A correctly marked worksite for triage.</li>
<li class="">A map of the sector showing the area covered by the assessment.</li>
<li class="">Development of the sector plan of action and priorities by <a class="term" data-tooltip="USAR Coordination Cell" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/ucc">UCC</a>.</li>
<li class="">Assignment of USAR teams to worksites.</li>
<li class="">Further resource requests.</li>
<li class="">Approved data in the <a class="term" data-tooltip="INSARAG Coordination and Management System" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/icms">ICMS</a> dashboard.</li>
<li class="">An up-to-date ICMS dashboard.</li>
</ol>
<h2 class="anchor anchorTargetStickyNavbar_Vzrq" id="asr-level-3-rapid-search-and-rescue">ASR Level 3: Rapid Search and Rescue<a href="#asr-level-3-rapid-search-and-rescue" class="hash-link" aria-label="Direct link to ASR Level 3: Rapid Search and Rescue" title="Direct link to ASR Level 3: Rapid Search and Rescue" translate="no">​</a></h2>
<p><strong>Definitions and purpose</strong></p>
<ul>
<li class="">Usually applies in the early stages of a large-scale event when a relatively small number of
teams are available compared to the number of sites that require search and rescue</li>
<li class="">Fairly rapid progress needed to ensure the allocated structures are all searched relatively
quickly to maximise the lifesaving opportunities</li>
<li class="">There is relatively modest commitment to each site with:<!-- -->
<ul>
<li class="">Use of physical, dog or technical search techniques</li>
<li class="">Rescue operations using debris removal and limited shoring, breaking and breaching etc.</li>
<li class="">Limited penetration into the structure/rubble</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li class="">The search and/or rescues are normally possible to complete within one operational period,
e.g. a few hours</li>
<li class="">A team will not normally undertake long term operations (more than one operational period) to
penetrate deeply into the structure unless there are strong indications of live victims</li>
<li class="">Deeply entombed victims may not be found during this level</li>
<li class="">At this level teams should identify those structures or worksites where a Level 4 search might
be worthwhile</li>
<li class="">If a confirmed deeply trapped live victim is identified, team may extend to Level 4 operations
if the assignment constraints and restraints allow or they get permission from sector coordination.
However, they must ensure Level 3 work is completed for the remaining worksites assigned to them
In case they are not able or do not complete the rescue, they should ask for additional resources</li>
<li class="">If additional rescue sites are identified at any time, then a new <a class="" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/sectorisation-and-zones#worksite-identification"><ins>worksite ID</ins></a> should be created</li>
<li class="">The SCC/<a class="term" data-tooltip="USAR Coordination Cell" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/ucc">UCC</a> must keep track of all ASR3 assignments and reassign to the site for ASR4, except
for those sites being reported not to need ASR4</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Carried out when and by who</strong></p>
<ol>
<li class="">This is usually done when <span class="term" data-tooltip="Urban Search and Rescue">USAR</span> teams are initially allocated into sectors</li>
<li class="">Should always be done at identified worksites</li>
<li class="">Done by <a class="" href="/capacity/usar-team-capacity/usar-teams#light-usar-team"><ins>Light</ins></a>, <a class="" href="/capacity/usar-team-capacity/usar-teams#medium-usar-team"><ins>Medium</ins></a>, <a class="" href="/capacity/usar-team-capacity/usar-teams#heavy-usar-team"><ins>Heavy</ins></a> <span class="term" data-tooltip="Urban Search and Rescue">USAR</span> teams</li>
<li class="">This work might also be done by the <a class="term" data-tooltip="Local Emergency Management Authority" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/coordination-elements#local-emergency-management-authority-lema">LEMA&#x27;s</a> national teams</li>
<li class="">One USAR team may be able to operate simultaneously at more than one worksite due to the
limited commitment</li>
</ol>
<p><strong><span class="term" data-tooltip="International Search and Rescue Advisory Group">INSARAG</span> Tools</strong></p>
<ol>
<li class=""><a class="" href="/operations/operational-procedures/worksite-triage#worksite-triage-form"><ins>Worksite Triage form</ins></a></li>
<li class=""><a class="" href="/operations/operational-procedures/worksite-report"><ins>Worksite Report form</ins></a></li>
<li class=""><a class="" href="/operations/operational-procedures/victim-extrication"><ins>Victim Extrication form</ins></a></li>
<li class=""><a class="" href="/operations/operational-procedures/marking-and-signalling#worksite-triage-marking"><ins>Worksite Marking</ins></a> system</li>
<li class="">
<a class="term" data-tooltip="INSARAG Coordination and Management System" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/icms">ICMS</a>
</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Outputs</strong></p>
<ol>
<li class="">Completed <a class="" href="/operations/operational-procedures/worksite-report"><ins>Worksite Reports</ins></a></li>
<li class="">Marking of worksites</li>
<li class="">Completed <a class="" href="/operations/operational-procedures/victim-extrication"><ins>Victim Extrication forms</ins></a></li>
</ol>
<h2 class="anchor anchorTargetStickyNavbar_Vzrq" id="asr-level-4-full-search-and-rescue">ASR Level 4: Full Search and Rescue<a href="#asr-level-4-full-search-and-rescue" class="hash-link" aria-label="Direct link to ASR Level 4: Full Search and Rescue" title="Direct link to ASR Level 4: Full Search and Rescue" translate="no">​</a></h2>
<p><strong>Definitions and purpose</strong></p>
<ul>
<li class="">This is the SAR work that should identify, locate and rescue the small number of heavily trapped
or entombed survivors that local rescuers, first responders, <a class="term" data-tooltip="Local Emergency Management Authority" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/coordination-elements#local-emergency-management-authority-lema">LEMA</a> resources or ASR Level 3 operations
did not achieve</li>
<li class="">Teams will penetrate most or all of the survivable voids</li>
<li class="">These are likely to be longer term (more than one operational period) operations requiring a wide
range of <span class="term" data-tooltip="Urban Search and Rescue">USAR</span> skills, e.g.:<!-- -->
<ul>
<li class="">All possible search techniques and equipment and often repeated as access is achieved</li>
<li class="">Possibly extensive shoring to make the structure or access routes safe</li>
<li class="">Heavy and repeated breaking and breaching of the full range of structural elements</li>
<li class="">Lifting and/or moving of large elements</li>
<li class="">Some delayering may take place at this level if access is needed to an identified potential
live rescue</li>
<li class="">Working in confined spaces, sometimes deep inside structures</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li class="">This may involve several teams on the same worksite</li>
<li class="">Complete command and control of the worksite is needed</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Carried out when and by who</strong></p>
<ol>
<li class="">This level is normally carried out after or in conjunction with Level 3 Rapid Search and Rescue.</li>
<li class="">If the <a class="term" data-tooltip="Local Emergency Management Authority" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/coordination-elements#local-emergency-management-authority-lema">LEMA</a> has identified specific sites already, a team may go straight into Level 4 operations
as their first tasking.</li>
<li class="">Carried out by <a class="" href="/capacity/usar-team-capacity/usar-teams#medium-usar-team"><ins>Medium</ins></a> and <a class="" href="/capacity/usar-team-capacity/usar-teams#heavy-usar-team"><ins>Heavy</ins></a> <span class="term" data-tooltip="Urban Search and Rescue">USAR</span> teams.</li>
</ol>

</li>

<li class="">Marking of worksites</li>
<li class="">Completed <a class="" href="/operations/operational-procedures/victim-extrication"><ins>Victim Extrication forms</ins></a></li>

<h2 class="anchor anchorTargetStickyNavbar_Vzrq" id="asr-level-5-total-coverage-search-and-recovery">ASR Level 5: Total Coverage Search and Recovery<a href="#asr-level-5-total-coverage-search-and-recovery" class="hash-link" aria-label="Direct link to ASR Level 5: Total Coverage Search and Recovery" title="Direct link to ASR Level 5: Total Coverage Search and Recovery" translate="no">​</a></h2>
<p><strong>Definitions and purpose</strong></p>
<ul>
<li class="">This usually means operations carried out at a worksite to recover the deceased victims</li>
<li class="">It is conceivably still part of the rescue phase if this is deemed necessary by the
coordinating authority</li>
<li class="">There may be a “miracle” find of a live victim that is achieved as the structure is de-layered
or deconstructed</li>
<li class="">If the work relates to collapsed structures or rubble pile worksites this work can include:<!-- -->
<ul>
<li class="">Searching or making access into every possible void</li>
<li class="">All the <span class="term" data-tooltip="Urban Search and Rescue">USAR</span> skills listed in Level 4</li>
<li class="">Delayering of large elements to allow access to all parts of the structure or rubble pile</li>
<li class="">Working with heavy machinery, e.g. cranes and demolition equipment, to achieve this access</li>
<li class="">Complete command and control of the worksite is essential</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Carried out when and by who</strong></p>
<ol>
<li class="">This is usually done after the rescue phase.</li>
<li class="">This level is not normally carried out by international <span class="term" data-tooltip="Urban Search and Rescue">USAR</span> teams.</li>
<li class="">Usually done by the <a class="term" data-tooltip="Local Emergency Management Authority" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/coordination-elements#local-emergency-management-authority-lema">LEMA</a> resources for their own body recovery purposes.</li>
<li class="">International USAR teams can be asked to undertake this task where area clearance and body
recovery is a high priority.</li>
<li class="">Some international teams may do this and some may not, each team will make their own decision.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong><span class="term" data-tooltip="International Search and Rescue Advisory Group">INSARAG</span> Tools</strong></p>
<ol>
<li class=""><a class="" href="/operations/operational-procedures/worksite-triage#worksite-triage-form"><ins>Worksite Triage form</ins></a></li>
<li class=""><a class="" href="/operations/operational-procedures/worksite-report"><ins>Worksite Report form</ins></a></li>
<li class=""><a class="" href="/operations/operational-procedures/marking-and-signalling#worksite-triage-marking"><ins>Worksite Marking</ins></a> system</li>
<li class=""><a class="" href="/operations/operational-procedures/victim-extrication"><ins>Victim Extrication form</ins></a></li>
<li class="">
<a class="term" data-tooltip="INSARAG Coordination and Management System" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/icms">ICMS</a>
</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Outputs</strong></p>
<ol>
<li class="">Completed <a class="" href="/operations/operational-procedures/worksite-report"><ins>Worksite Reports</ins></a></li>
<li class=""><a class="" href="/operations/operational-procedures/marking-and-signalling#worksite-triage-marking"><ins>Worksite Marking</ins></a></li>
<li class="">Completed <a class="" href="/operations/operational-procedures/victim-extrication"><ins>Victim Extrication forms</ins></a></li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Area Clearance to ASR Level 5</strong></p>
<ul>
<li class="">This level can also apply to areas where building collapse is less or absent, but USAR skills are
needed for access or safety to enable complete clearance of all possible victims. In this case the
operations will include:<!-- -->
<ul>
<li class="">Systematic search of every room of every structure in the assigned area of operation</li>
<li class="">This operation should quickly clear relatively large areas</li>
<li class="">If necessary forced entry is used to gain access to all areas</li>
<li class="">Heavy machinery may occasionally be needed to clear smaller rubble piles</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li class="">This may specifically be for deceased victim location/recovery</li>
<li class="">The rules of assignment (brief) must detail what teams do if they locate a live or deceased
victim, e.g. call in other teams or stay and deal with it themselves</li>
<li class="">Full control and coordination are needed with detailed recording by the teams of the exact areas
they have cleared</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Carried out when and by who</strong></p>
<ol>
<li class=""><a class="term" data-tooltip="Local Emergency Management Authority" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/coordination-elements#local-emergency-management-authority-lema">LEMA</a> resources usually do this but international USAR teams may be asked to do this in
some circumstances</li>
<li class="">USAR teams may or may not decide to move to this phase of work based on various factors e.g.
other rescue possibilities, capacity of teams at the event, policy of the team, mandate from sponsors etc.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong><span class="term" data-tooltip="International Search and Rescue Advisory Group">INSARAG</span> Tools</strong></p>
<ol>
<li class=""><a class="" href="/operations/operational-procedures/victim-extrication"><ins>Victim Extrication form</ins></a> if necessary</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Outputs</strong></p>
<ol>
<li class="">Report on the work carried out as agreed with the <a class="term" data-tooltip="USAR Coordination Cell" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/ucc">UCC</a>/<span class="term" data-tooltip="On-Site Operations Coordination Centre">OSOCC</span>/<a class="term" data-tooltip="Local Emergency Management Authority" href="/coordination/international-usar-coordination/coordination-elements#local-emergency-management-authority-lema">LEMA</a></li>
<li class="">Maps of areas cleared as part of reporting results</li>
